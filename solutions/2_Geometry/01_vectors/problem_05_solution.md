# Exercise 5. Angle Between Vectors

## Given Vectors

We are given:

$$
u=(1,1,0), \quad v=(1,0,1)
$$

We need to predict the type of angle using the dot product, calculate the angle, and compare it with our prediction.

## Solution

### 1. Predicting the Type of Angle

First, I calculate the dot product:

$$
u \cdot v = 1(1)+1(0)+0(1)
$$

$$
u \cdot v = 1
$$

Since the dot product is positive, the angle between the vectors is acute (less than 90 degrees).

### 2. Calculating the Angle

I use the formula:

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{u\cdot v}{|u||v|}
$$

First, I calculate the lengths of both vectors:

$$
|u|=\sqrt{1^2+1^2+0^2}=\sqrt{2}
$$

$$
|v|=\sqrt{1^2+0^2+1^2}=\sqrt{2}
$$

Now I put the values into the formula:

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2}}
$$

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{1}{2}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\theta=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)
$$

$$
\boxed{\theta=60^\circ}
$$

### 3. Comparing the Result

The calculated angle is 60 degrees.

This agrees with my first prediction because 60 degrees is an acute angle.

## Conclusion

The angle between the two vectors is 60 degrees. The positive dot product helped me predict the type of angle before calculating it.
