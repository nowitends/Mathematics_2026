# Exercise 4. Dot Product and Perpendicularity

## Given Vectors

We are given:

$$
u=(1,2,-1)
$$

$$
v=(2,-1,3)
$$

We need to calculate the dot product, check whether the vectors are perpendicular, and find a value of $a$ such that $w=(2,-1,a)$ is perpendicular to $u$.

## Solution

### 1. Calculate the Dot Product

I use the dot product formula:

$$
u\cdot v=u_1v_1+u_2v_2+u_3v_3
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
u\cdot v=1(2)+2(-1)+(-1)(3)
$$

$$
=2-2-3
$$

$$
\boxed{u\cdot v=-3}
$$

### 2. Check Perpendicularity

Two vectors are perpendicular if their dot product is zero.

Here:

$$
u\cdot v=-3\neq0
$$

Therefore, the vectors are not perpendicular.

### 3. Find the Value of a

Now we have:

$$
w=(2,-1,a)
$$

We want $w$ to be perpendicular to $u$.

For perpendicular vectors, the dot product must be zero:

$$
u\cdot w=0
$$

Substituting the components:

$$
1(2)+2(-1)+(-1)(a)=0
$$

$$
2-2-a=0
$$

$$
-a=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{a=0}
$$

### 4. Check the Result

For $a=0$, the vector becomes:

$$
w=(2,-1,0)
$$

Now I calculate the dot product again:

$$
u\cdot w=1(2)+2(-1)+(-1)(0)
$$

$$
=2-2+0
$$

$$
\boxed{u\cdot w=0}
$$

The result is correct because the dot product is zero.

## Conclusion

The dot product of the original vectors is:

$$
\boxed{u\cdot v=-3}
$$

Therefore, they are not perpendicular.

The required value is:

$$
\boxed{a=0}
$$

With this value, the vectors $u$ and $w$ are perpendicular.
