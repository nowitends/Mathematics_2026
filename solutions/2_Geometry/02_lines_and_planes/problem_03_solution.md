# Exercise 3. Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector

## Given

We are given:

$$
P=(1,-2,3)
$$

$$
n=(2,1,-1)
$$

We need to find the equation of the plane.

## Solution

### 1. Finding the Plane Equation

I use the plane formula:

$$
n\cdot((x,y,z)-P)=0
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
2(x-1)+(y+2)-(z-3)=0
$$

Expanding:

$$
2x-2+y+2-z+3=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{2x+y-z+3=0}
$$

### 2. Checking the Point

I substitute P=(1,-2,3):

$$
2(1)+(-2)-3+3=0
$$

$$
2-2-3+3=0
$$

The result is zero, so the point satisfies the equation.

### 3. Role of the Normal Vector

The normal vector is perpendicular to the plane.

It shows the direction that is perpendicular to every direction lying in the plane.

## Conclusion

The equation of the plane is:

$$
\boxed{2x+y-z+3=0}
$$

The given point satisfies the equation.
