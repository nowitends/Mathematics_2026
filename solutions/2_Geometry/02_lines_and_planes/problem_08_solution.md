# Exercise 8. Intersection Point of a Line and a Plane

## Given

The line is:

$$
(x,y,z)=(1,0,2)+t(1,2,-1)
$$

The plane is:

$$
x+y+z=4
$$

We need to find the intersection point and check the result.

## Solution

### 1. Writing the Line Coordinates

From the line equation:

$$
x=1+t
$$

$$
y=2t
$$

$$
z=2-t
$$

### 2. Finding the Intersection

I substitute these values into the plane equation:

$$
x+y+z=4
$$

$$
(1+t)+2t+(2-t)=4
$$

Simplifying:

$$
3+2t=4
$$

$$
2t=1
$$

$$
\boxed{t=\frac12}
$$

### 3. Finding the Intersection Point

I substitute t=1/2 into the line:

$$
x=1+\frac12=\frac32
$$

$$
y=2\cdot\frac12=1
$$

$$
z=2-\frac12=\frac32
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{P=\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right)}
$$

### 4. Checking the Result

First, I check the line:

$$
(1,0,2)+\frac12(1,2,-1)
$$

$$
=\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right)
$$

Now I check the plane:

$$
\frac32+1+\frac32=4
$$

Both descriptions give the same point.

## Conclusion

The intersection point is:

$$
\boxed{P=\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right)}
$$

The point satisfies both the line and the plane equations.
