# Exercise 1. Line Through Two Points

## Given

We are given two points:

$$
A=(1,2), \quad B=(4,-1)
$$

We need to find the parametric and general form of the line.

## Solution

### 1. Finding the Direction Vector

I subtract point A from point B:

$$
v=B-A
$$

$$
v=(4-1,-1-2)
$$

$$
\boxed{v=(3,-3)}
$$

### 2. Parametric Form

The parametric form is:

$$
(x,y)=A+tv
$$

Substituting the values:

$$
(x,y)=(1,2)+t(3,-3)
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{x=1+3t}
$$

$$
\boxed{y=2-3t}
$$

### 3. General Form

The direction vector is (3,-3). A normal vector is:

$$
n=(1,1)
$$

Using point A:

$$
1(x-1)+1(y-2)=0
$$

$$
\boxed{x+y-3=0}
$$

### 4. Checking Both Points

For A=(1,2):

$$
1+2-3=0
$$

For B=(4,-1):

$$
4-1-3=0
$$

Both points satisfy the equation.

## Conclusion

The direction vector is (3,-3).

The normal vector is (1,1).

The parametric form is:

$$
(x,y)=(1,2)+t(3,-3)
$$

The general form is:

$$
\boxed{x+y-3=0}
$$
