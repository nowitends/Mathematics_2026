# Exercise 2. A Line in Space

## Given

The line is:

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2)
$$

We need to find a point, its direction vector, check P, and find the point for t=-1.

## Solution

### 1. Point and Direction Vector

From the equation, one point on the line is:

$$
\boxed{A=(2,-1,3)}
$$

The direction vector is:

$$
\boxed{v=(1,2,-2)}
$$

### 2. Checking if P Lies on the Line

We are given:

$$
P=(4,3,-1)
$$

I compare the coordinates:

$$
2+t=4 \Rightarrow t=2
$$

$$
-1+2t=3 \Rightarrow t=2
$$

$$
3-2t=-1 \Rightarrow t=2
$$

All three equations give the same value of t.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{P \text{ lies on the line}}
$$

### 3. Finding the Point for t=-1

I substitute t=-1:

$$
(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+(-1)(1,2,-2)
$$

$$
=(2,-1,3)+(-1,-2,2)
$$

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(1,-3,5)}
$$

## Conclusion

The point is (2,-1,3), and the direction vector is (1,2,-2).

P lies on the line because all coordinates give t=2.

For t=-1, the point is:

$$
\boxed{(1,-3,5)}
$$
