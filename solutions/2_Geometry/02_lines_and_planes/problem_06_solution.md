# Exercise 6. Angle Between Planes

## Given

We are given two planes:

$$
\Pi_1:x+y+z=1
$$

$$
\Pi_2:x-y=2
$$

We need to check if they are parallel and find the angle between them.

## Solution

### 1. Finding the Normal Vectors

From the plane equations, the normal vectors are:

$$
n_1=(1,1,1)
$$

$$
n_2=(1,-1,0)
$$

### 2. Checking if the Planes Are Parallel

The normal vectors are not scalar multiples of each other.

Therefore, the planes are not parallel.

### 3. Calculating the Angle

I use the formula:

$$
\cos\theta=
\frac{|n_1\cdot n_2|}
{|n_1||n_2|}
$$

First, I calculate the dot product:

$$
n_1\cdot n_2
=1(1)+1(-1)+1(0)
$$

$$
=1-1+0=0
$$

Now:

$$
\cos\theta=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{\theta=90^\circ}
$$

### 4. Explanation

The normal vectors are perpendicular because their dot product is zero.

This means that the two planes meet at a right angle.

## Conclusion

The planes are not parallel.

The angle between them is:

$$
\boxed{90^\circ}
$$
