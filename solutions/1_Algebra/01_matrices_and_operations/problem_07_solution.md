Exercise 7. Identity Matrix, Zero Matrix, and Powers

        1. AI = A
        2. IA = A
        3. A + 0 = A
        4. A² = [[4, 4], [0, 4]]
        5. A³ = [[8, 12], [0, 8]]

        The identity matrix keeps A the same when multiplying. Adding zero also keeps A the same. For A² and A³, I multiply A by itself.
