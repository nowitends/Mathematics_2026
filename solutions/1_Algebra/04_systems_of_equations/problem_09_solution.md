# Exercise 9. A System from a Sum Condition

## Given Conditions

We need to find $x$ and $y$ satisfying:

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=10\\
2x+5y=32
\end{cases}
$$

The task is to solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

### Step 1: Eliminate One Variable

First, I write the equations:

$$
x+y=10
$$

$$
2x+5y=32
$$

I multiply the first equation by $-2$:

$$
-2x-2y=-20
$$

Now I add it to the second equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x+5y&=32\\
-2x-2y&=-20\\
\hline
3y&=12
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore:

$$
y=4
$$

### Step 2: Find x

I substitute $y=4$ into the first equation:

$$
x+y=10
$$

$$
x+4=10
$$

$$
x=6
$$

The solution is:

$$
\boxed{x=6,\quad y=4}
$$

### Step 3: Check the Result

I substitute the values into both original equations.

First equation:

$$
6+4=10
$$

Second equation:

$$
2(6)+5(4)=32
$$

$$
12+20=32
$$

Both equations are correct.

### Geometric Explanation

Each equation represents a straight line.

The first equation can be written as:

$$
y=10-x
$$

The second equation can be written as:

$$
y=\frac{32-2x}{5}
$$

The slopes of these lines are different:

$$
m_1=-1,\qquad m_2=-\frac{2}{5}
$$

Since the slopes are different, the lines intersect at exactly one point.

This is why the system has a unique solution.

## Conclusion

The two lines intersect at:

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(6,4)}
$$

Therefore, the system has exactly one solution.
