# Exercise 15 - Combining Determinant Properties

## Question

Given invertible 2x2 matrices A and B:

\[
\det(A)=-2,\quad \det(B)=3
\]

Calculate:

\[
\det(B^{-1}A^TB)
\]

## Solution

Using the determinant properties:

\[
\det(B^{-1}A^TB)
=\det(B^{-1})\det(A^T)\det(B)
\]

Since:

\[
\det(B^{-1})=\frac{1}{\det(B)}=\frac{1}{3}
\]

and:

\[
\det(A^T)=\det(A)=-2
\]

Then:

\[
\det(B^{-1}A^TB)
=\frac{1}{3}(-2)(3)
=-2
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\det(B^{-1}A^TB)=-2}
\]

The factors involving B cancel because the determinant of an inverse is the reciprocal of the original determinant.
