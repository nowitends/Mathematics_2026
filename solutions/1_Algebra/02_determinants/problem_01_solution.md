# Exercise 1 - Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix

## Question

Calculate the determinants of the following matrices and determine which matrix is invertible.

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4
\end{pmatrix}
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Solution

For a 2x2 matrix, the determinant is calculated using the formula:

$$
\det(A) = ad - bc
$$

### 1. Determinant of Matrix A

For matrix A:

$$
\det(A) = 3 \cdot 4 - (-2) \cdot 5
$$

$$
= 12 + 10
$$

$$
= 22
$$

### 2. Determinant of Matrix B

For matrix B:

$$
\det(B) = 1 \cdot 14 - 7 \cdot 2
$$

$$
= 14 - 14
$$

$$
= 0
$$

## Conclusion

A square matrix is invertible if and only if its determinant is nonzero.

Since:

$$
\det(A) = 22 \neq 0
$$

Matrix A is invertible.

Since:

$$
\det(B) = 0
$$

Matrix B is not invertible.

Therefore, matrix A is invertible, while matrix B is not.
